# Noklingo v3 curriculum overview

The planned first curriculum is twelve ordered video lessons for practical
spoken Thai. Only lessons backed by a locally supplied MP4 and a verified
transcript may be enabled; an unverified title or recording is not course
content.

## Learner journey

```text
Today
  -> watch the active lesson video
  -> study 5-10 cue cards
  -> complete the diagnostic
  -> return on the next local day
  -> retrieve the cards
  -> score at least 9/10 on active questions
  -> unlock the next lesson
```

The next lesson can be introduced on the same day that the prior lesson is
mastered. It still cannot be mastered before a later local day.

Up to three due questions from mastered lessons are interleaved into a mastery
session. Their results update item schedules independently and never change the
active lesson's 9/10 gate or relock completed material. Successful delayed recall
uses approximate intervals of 2, 5, 12, and 30 days; lapses return tomorrow.

## Product surfaces

- **Today** explains the single next action: new lesson, mastery due, waiting, or
  curriculum complete.
- **Study** contains video, cue cards, retrieval cards, and quizzes.
- **Results** reveals delayed-mastery answers, corrections, and the unlock state.
- **Library** replays the active and mastered lessons without learning side
  effects; future videos remain locked.
- **Progress** shows lesson mastery, due items, streak, and recent accuracy.
- **Settings** controls audio, volume, Romanization, Thai script, dark mode,
  reduced motion, preferred polite particle, v3 export/import, and reset.

There is no unit path, XP economy, heart recovery, checkpoint gate, achievement
tree, or standalone practice destination in v3.

## Content progression

Lessons should move from high-frequency social exchanges toward longer natural
responses while continuously retrieving prior phrases. Every lesson must mix
listening, situation/response, meaning recognition, and phrase recall.
Romanization remains a scaffold, Thai script remains available for accuracy, and
the scored path never requires Thai literacy.

The authoring backlog is not permission to invent missing dialogue. Add each of
the remaining lessons only after its media, transcript, captions, cue cards, and
quiz variants meet the [authoring contract](./COURSE_AUTHORING.md).
